Overview

MoraWorld turns agent-built 3D scenes into controllable, photorealistic robot rollouts. One world model takes inputs from action maps to full grey-box scenes, with long-horizon generation and live control across embodiments. Physics-grounded scene variations support iterative policy training and evaluation, from simulation to real robots.
